Welcome to Tab Sleeper!

Your browser is about to get a lot faster. Tab Sleeper automatically unloads tabs you aren't using to save CPU and RAM.

⏱️
Auto-Sleep Tabs sleep after 5 minutes of inactivity. Just click the tab to instantly wake it up!
🎧
Smart Detection We never sleep pinned tabs or tabs playing music/video.
⌨️
Shortcut & Popup Use Ctrl+Shift+S to instantly sleep your current tab, or use the popup to sleep all other tabs!
